The bar we are clearing

What "Goldman-level" actually means

Retail tools describe a stock. Institutional research takes a position — an out-of-consensus, falsifiable, priced, and accountable view. Six things separate the two, and every one of them is a capability the agent must own, not decorate.

Differentiated view

Variant perception

A thesis is only worth writing where it differs from the Street. The agent explicitly models consensus and states where — and why — it disagrees. No variance, no note.

Rigour

A real model underneath

Driver-based, three-statement forecasts that tie out. Revenue built bottom-up from units × price × mix, not a growth-rate guess. The price target is an output of the model, not a vibe.

Triangulation

Valued three ways

DCF, sum-of-the-parts, and peer multiples must roughly agree — and when they don't, the gap is the insight. Every target carries a bull / base / bear tree with probabilities.

Evidence

Primary research

Reads the actual 10-K / annual report and the concall transcript, cross-checks channel and alternative data, and cites the page and line — never launders a secondary summary as fact.

Risk

Knows what kills the thesis

Names the two or three monitorables that would flip the call, the factor and crowding exposure, and the forensic red flags — before the market forces the question.

Accountability

A track record

Every call is dated, priced, and scored against outcome. The agent's coverage memory means it can be wrong on the record — and learn from the miss — the way a covering analyst is.

01
What it can do

Capability map

Ten capability blocks. The first nine map onto the analytical lenses you already have; the rest are the agentic layer that turns analysis into research.

Coverage & universe
Maintain a coverage list with sector expertise and a curated comps universe; screen and triage new names; decide which deserve depth.
Financial modelling
Build and maintain 3-statement models — driver-based revenue, margin bridges, working capital, capex, debt schedules — with scenario and sensitivity toggles.
Valuation
Two-stage DCF, sum-of-the-parts, residual income, and relative multiples; reconciled into a scenario-weighted 12-month price target.
Estimates & consensus
Forecast quarterly/annual revenue, margins and EPS; compare to Street consensus; run earnings previews and reviews with beat/miss attribution.
Thesis construction
Assemble a differentiated thesis: variant perception, the key debates, the catalyst calendar, and the falsifiers — a bull case and a bear case that both hold water.
Industry & thematic
TAM sizing, value-chain and competitive-structure mapping, structural-vs-cyclical framing, regulatory reads, and cross-sector thematic work.
Primary & alt-data
Ingest filings and transcripts in full; fold in channel checks, expert-network notes, and alternative data (card spend, web/app traffic, hiring, satellite) where available.
Forensic & quality
Piotroski / Altman / Beneish, accruals and cash-vs-profit, related-party and pledging checks — a gate the thesis must pass before a buy rating is allowed.
Risk & positioning
Factor exposures, short interest, ownership and crowdedness, liquidity, and the explicit downside scenario — how the position behaves when it's wrong.
Communication
Produce the actual products: quick take, tearsheet, deep-dive, initiation note, earnings flash, thematic — each in house voice, every claim sourced.
02
What it must understand

Knowledge domains

Capabilities are verbs; these are the nouns behind them — the body of understanding the agent draws on. Sector fluency is where "generic finance bot" and "analyst" visibly diverge.

Foundations

Accounting & the statements

GAAP / IFRS / Ind-AS, revenue recognition, leases and off-balance-sheet, one-offs vs recurring, how the three statements articulate — and where numbers are massaged.

Foundations

Corporate finance & valuation theory

WACC and CAPM, cost of capital, terminal value, the ROIC-vs-WACC spread as the engine of value creation, capital allocation, and what actually drives a multiple.

The edge

Sector-specific KPIs & structure

The metrics that matter per industry — banks (NIM, CASA, NPA, credit cost), SaaS (NRR, CAC payback, Rule of 40), retail (SSSG, density), commodities (cost curve), pharma (pipeline, patent cliff), utilities (rate base). Each sector is its own dialect.

Context

Macro & the cycle

Rates, inflation, FX, credit and liquidity conditions, commodity cycles, and where a sector sits in its own cycle — the top-down that reconciles with the bottom-up.

Context

Market structure & behaviour

How flows, positioning, index events and ownership move price; consensus formation and the behavioural biases that create the mispricings research exists to exploit.

Discipline

Governance, regulation & statistics

SEBI / SEC disclosure regimes, board and promoter governance, related-party risk; and the quantitative methods — regression, attribution, scenario/Monte-Carlo — to keep the reasoning honest.

Set before any data is pulled

The research mandate

Before the agent pulls a single datapoint it fixes three coordinates. Together they are the mandate — and they reshape what gets researched, how it's framed, and what lands on the page. Same engine, a different analyst each time.

Axis 1 · Side

Sell-side or buy-side

A recommendation for clients, or a capital-allocation decision for a book. Reframes the whole output.

Axis 2 · Market

Geography & regime

Americas, Europe, Asia, Japan, Australia, EM — each a swappable accounting / regulatory / macro profile.

Axis 3 · Depth

L0 → L5

How far down to drill — snapshot to living coverage. The ladder in the next section.

Sell-side and buy-side share ~80% of the machinery — the same lenses, the same model. The mandate re-weights and re-frames the last mile: what the note argues, how conviction is expressed, and which risks lead.

→
04 · Critique

Red-team & source gate

Non-negotiables

Discipline & guardrails

Honesty

Insufficient evidence beats invention

Inherits the engine's rule: a lens with missing data says so; the agent never fabricates a number, a source, or a channel check. Confidence is stated, not implied.

Provenance

Every figure carries its source

Filing page, transcript line, provider, or "illustrative snapshot." A claim without a traceable source doesn't enter the note.

Gate

Forensics can veto a rating

A Beneish/accruals/pledging red flag caps the rating regardless of how cheap or strong the name looks — the value-trap defence, enforced.

Boundary

Decision support, not advice

It analyses; it does not place orders and it is not investment advice. Real decisions are verified against primary filings — and the product says so on every page.

The one line that defines the whole thing: a Goldman-level note is not a longer summary — it is a falsifiable, priced, sourced disagreement with the consensus, that its author is accountable for.

Everything in this blueprint exists to let the agent produce exactly that, at whichever of the six depths you dial in.
